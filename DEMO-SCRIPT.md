# GridSmith — Demo Video Script (2–4 minutes)

Format: screen recording of the live app with voice-over. Total runtime ≈ 3 minutes.
Narration is written to be read aloud at a natural pace (~130 words/minute).

---

## Segment 1 — The hook (0:00–0:30)

**On screen:** A clock showing evening; a parent/teacher at a table with a printed
curriculum grid and a blank notebook. Cut to the GridSmith homepage.

**Narration:**
> "It's nine in the evening. Tomorrow there's a test, and Ibu Sari has just received the
> curriculum grid from her daughter's school — four topics, in Indonesian, on one sheet
> of paper. Now she has to turn that grid into good practice questions… by hand.
> This is the problem GridSmith solves."

## Segment 2 — Paste the grid (0:30–1:05)

**On screen:** In GridSmith: set **Grade 4**, **Mathematics**. Paste into the
"kisi-kisi" box:

```
Penjumlahan dan pengurangan
Pecahan sederhana
Perkalian dan pembagian
Kehidupan di laut dalam
```

The live "Grid preview" appears under the box.

**Narration:**
> "She pastes the grid straight into GridSmith — in Indonesian, exactly as the school
> wrote it. Watch the preview: GridSmith reads each line. 'Pecahan sederhana' becomes
> Fractions. 'Perkalian dan pembagian' becomes Multiplication and Division.
> And the last line — 'deep-sea life' — matches no topic, so GridSmith doesn't throw it
> away. It becomes a theme for word problems."

## Segment 3 — Generate the set (1:05–1:45)

**On screen:** Drag the difficulty sliders (Easy 4, Medium 4, Hard 2), choose **10
questions**, click **Generate practice set**. Scroll the worksheet: fraction bar
question, division, a themed word problem. Click **New numbers** once to show the
questions re-randomize.

**Narration:**
> "She chooses the difficulty mix — some easy wins, some stretch questions — and presses
> Generate. Ten questions, built from her grid, in about a second. A fraction bar to
> read, division to solve, and a word problem that actually uses her daughter's
> deep-sea theme. Wrong set? One click — new numbers, new names, new stories.
> No two worksheets are ever the same."

## Segment 4 — The answer key (1:45–2:25)

**On screen:** Click **Show answer key**. Scroll through two or three entries slowly:
a fraction comparison and a multi-step problem, showing the step-by-step working.

**Narration:**
> "And here is the part parents ask for most: a full answer key — not just answers,
> but the working. Column by column for addition. Common denominators for fractions.
> Every step written the way a teacher would explain it at the whiteboard, so checking
> homework takes minutes, and a parent can actually teach from it."

## Segment 5 — English & print (2:25–2:55)

**On screen:** Switch subject to **English Language**, Grade 5, Generate: show the
reading passage and comprehension questions. Then click **Print / Save PDF**; show the
print preview: student worksheet first, answer key starting on its own page.

**Narration:**
> "GridSmith speaks English too — vocabulary, grammar, and original reading passages
> with comprehension questions. When she's ready, one click prints a clean worksheet
> with name and score lines for the student… and the answer key follows on its own
> pages, only if she wants it."

## Segment 6 — Closing impact (2:55–3:10)

**On screen:** The printed worksheet on the table next to the curriculum grid.
End card: GridSmith logo + "Practice sets, forged from your curriculum grid."

**Narration:**
> "From a sheet of topics to a finished, leveled practice set — in under a minute,
> for free, with no account and no internet. GridSmith: tonight's homework, handled."

---

### Production notes

- Record at 1080p with the browser at ~1365px wide so the control panel and worksheet
  are both readable.
- Use the app's own demo data: the grid above is the exact sample used in
  `index.html?demo=grid`, so the recording can be rehearsed in one click.
- Keep mouse movements slow on Segments 3–4; the step-by-step explanations are the
  product's strongest moment — give viewers time to read one full example.
